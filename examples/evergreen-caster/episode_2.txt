In Common Lisp, a function is a value. You can pass it as an argument or return it from another function. A closure can keep access to a lexical binding from the place where it was created. In Evergreen Compose, a button callback can close over the identifier of a story. When you tap the button, the callback selects that story. Give state a clear owner, capture the values the callback needs, and keep the view function easy to read.
